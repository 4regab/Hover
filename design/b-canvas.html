What this fixes

  • Screenshots sit on a dark panel, so a white screenshot reads as a picture instead of blending into the chrome. This is the direction to pick if the tray matters more to you than the notes.
  • The note's first line is its title. No more "Careerdog tasks" printed twice, once in the header and once in the body.
  • Every action lives in one hover toolbar. The note footer disappears, so the text area is the whole note.
  • Annotate gets a visible "Mark up" button on each picture instead of being buried.
  • Pictures get a name, a time and a size.

The risk

  • A dark note is not a sticky note. If the paper feeling is the point of Hover, this throws it away.
  • Eight paper colours become eight accent colours, which is a much weaker way to tell notes apart at a glance.
  • A floating toolbar over text needs care: it must never cover the line you are typing. It sits top-right, and the first line is the title, which is short.

Motion

  • The toolbar scales from 0.97 anchored top right, the corner it lives in, over 180ms.
  • Delete and Mark up fade in per picture, 180ms, no stagger — they belong to the one picture you are pointing at.
  • Tabs slide 8px out on hover, 160ms, transform only.
  • Nothing animates on a keyboard shortcut.

Cost to build

  • Middle of the three. The blur behind the toolbar and the tabs needs Avalonia's acrylic, which exists but costs a little on old machines. A flat 92% fill is the fallback.
