What this fixes

  • The purple no longer fights the text. Tint #EAE4F2 under #37352F instead of #D9C7FA under near-black.
  • Eleven controls in the note footer become three: one colour swatch, Archive, and an overflow menu. Delete stops being a peer of Archive.
  • The three unlabelled header glyphs appear on hover and carry tooltips with their shortcuts.
  • The tray's permanent dark delete circles appear on hover instead of sitting on top of every picture.
  • Pictures get a name and a time, grouped by day, so you can tell them apart.
  • The empty tray says what to do instead of showing a blank row with a stray delete button.

Motion

  • Hover reveals: 180ms, cubic-bezier(.23,1,.32,1), opacity and transform only.
  • Presses: scale(0.94–0.97) over 140ms.
  • The colour palette and the overflow menu scale from 0.96, anchored to the control that opened them, never from the centre of the note.
  • Nothing animates on a keyboard shortcut. Ctrl+Alt+N puts a note on screen with no transition at all.

Cost to build

  • Lowest of the three. Same layout, new colours, hover states, and a menu.
  • Needs a real menu popup and tooltips, neither of which exists yet.
